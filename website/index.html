About
The First Step

The First Step is a biophilic design strategy for neuro-inclusive classrooms. Most learning spaces were built without sensory needs in mind, and most cannot be rebuilt. So we start with the rooms that already exist.

Adjustable lighting, muted colour and living plants bring nature indoors. They lower sensory load for neurodivergent students and staff, and make the room calmer for everyone in it.

How it Works

From any classroom to a calmer one.

  1. Library

    Start with a room like yours. Ten classroom types give you a starting point in seconds.

  2. Conditions

    Tell the toolkit what the room is like. Daylight, lighting, glare, plants, walls and size.

  3. Recommendations

    Get recommendations for lighting, plants and colour, matched to the room. Adjust each one to suit.

  4. Plan & Impact

    See the plan on the floor, with the cost and energy case beside it. Save it, export it or send it for review.

  5. Light Guidelines

    Every recommendation traces back to a clear guideline. Light that is steady, warm and adjustable.

  6. Plant Guidelines

    Easy-care greenery, placed where it is seen and kept clear of paths.

  7. Colour & Material Guidelines

    Warm neutrals, muted greens and low-sheen finishes that keep the room settled.

Every room teaches the next one.

A room can meet every accessibility standard and still overwhelm the people in it. The First Step brings nature indoors through light, colour and plants, so the room supports every kind of mind.
